EDUCATION · INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE · PARENT JOURNEY

VAS Education

A school website redesign that shifts the organizing logic from “what the institution has to say” to “what a family needs to decide next.”

01 / CONTEXT

The content was not missing — the path through it was

VAS Education is an independent redesign concept for a school website with a broad information ecosystem: learning stages, programs, campuses, student life, outcomes, stories and admissions. I worked across product framing, information architecture, UX writing direction, UI/UX design, responsive interaction and front-end implementation.

Primary audienceProspective familiesHigh-consideration school choice.
Core problemInformation without a pathUseful content can still be hard to navigate.
Design modelQuestion-led journeyOrganize around what families need next.
Evidence statusPrototype + audit inputsNo parent research outcome claimed.
02 / AUDIENCE QUESTIONS

Different questions become important at different moments

01 / First impressionCan I trust this school?

Brand promise and credible proof need to arrive before curriculum complexity.

02 / FitWhich path fits my child?

Learning stages and pathways must be understandable as a connected journey.

03 / RealityWhat is daily life like?

Campus, teachers, student experience and parent voices make the promise concrete.

04 / ActionWhat do I do next?

Admissions, visits and questions need a calm next step rather than an abrupt form-first ending.

03 / ARCHITECTURE SHIFT

From content inventory to a guided decision sequence

The homepage was structured as a progressive argument: establish the promise, substantiate it, help families orient within the learning journey, show everyday experience and only then ask for action. This is a sequencing decision, not simply a new visual layout.

04 / INTERFACE PROOF

The interface keeps warmth, but uses structure to carry complexity

RESPONSIVE INTERFACE MODEL

Trust before complexity

Editorial entry and proof precede program detail to reduce the cognitive burden of the first visit.

Journey over catalogue

Learning stages are presented as a relationship rather than isolated pages that families must assemble mentally.

Progressive disclosure

Campus, pathways, stories and admissions become deeper only when the context makes them useful.

05 / OUTCOME & BOUNDARY

A clearer prototype for a complex education decision

The implemented experience connects brand, proof, program choice, campus context and admissions in one continuous journey. It demonstrates a stronger information model and responsive interface direction, not measured admissions performance.

NOT CLAIMED

No parent interviews, moderated usability recordings, enquiry data or conversion metrics were produced for this concept. The next validation should test three real family tasks: identify an appropriate pathway, compare campus options and book a visit after exploring the homepage.

06 / REFLECTION

Inspiration and decision support work better when they are sequenced

Education websites need both emotion and practical orientation. The redesign became clearer when those modes stopped competing inside the same block. The next iteration should validate the information architecture with parents and make program/campus comparison more explicit.
