ELECTRIC MOBILITY · PRODUCT + CORPORATE IA

VOLTIS

A bilingual EV brand prototype designed to make technology, product choice and company credibility read as one coherent system.

01 / CONTEXT

A company site that also has to sell the product idea

VOLTIS is a self-initiated electric-mobility website prototype built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The brief required more than a vehicle landing page: company story, product models, projects, news, resources, careers, media and contact all had to live in one coherent English/Vietnamese system. I owned the research synthesis, information architecture, visual direction, component/state system, responsive behavior and implementation.

ScopeBrand + product + corporateMultiple page roles, one system.
Primary pathUnderstand → compare → trust → actFrom brand promise to model intent and contact.
System pressureVI / EN responsive contentLayout must survive localization.
Evidence statusPrototype verifiedNo commercial product claims.
02 / DESIGN PROBLEM

Automotive polish is useless if the information model feels like a campaign microsite

The core tension was balancing cinematic product presentation with the credibility expected from a technology company. I structured the experience so the hero establishes the brand thesis, model sections create product contrast, and corporate/project/resource sections progressively answer whether the company feels real, capable and contactable.

01 / PositionClarify the brand thesis

Electric mobility is framed around energy, technology and human experience instead of feature dumping.

02 / CompareGive models distinct jobs

Concept vehicles represent different mobility needs rather than cosmetic variants of the same card.

03 / Build trustExpose the company system

Projects, resources, news, careers and company content turn the site from campaign surface into an ecosystem.

04 / Convert intentKeep test-drive/contact routes visible

Primary actions stay familiar while visual storytelling carries the brand.

03 / SYSTEM & DELIVERY

The useful proof is in states, localization and responsive pressure

CONCEPT ASSET / FALLBACK

Bilingual architecture

Vietnamese-first content and an English switch are built into the prototype so text expansion and label changes are part of the layout contract.

Component proof

The project includes dedicated design-system, component-state, sitemap, user-flow and prototype screens instead of treating the homepage as the entire design artifact.

Accessibility & motion

Native controls, visible focus states, Escape-close mobile navigation and reduced-motion support keep the premium layer compatible with basic interaction quality.

04 / OUTCOME & BOUNDARY

What the prototype demonstrates

The delivered prototype demonstrates a multi-route automotive web system with responsive typography, bilingual interaction, reusable visual grammar and implementation-ready behavior. Supporting design documentation records research, benchmark synthesis, visual direction, responsive strategy, localization rules, UX review and QA decisions.

NOT CLAIMED

VOLTIS, AERO and TERRAIN are concept material, not announced commercial vehicles. Reference photography includes third-party automotive imagery and must be replaced with verified production assets for real commercial use. No test-drive conversion, lead-generation or brand-lift metrics are claimed.

05 / REFLECTION

A mature visual system should make very different page roles feel related without making them identical

The project strengthened my understanding of system-level art direction: model storytelling can be expressive, while resources, careers and contact need more informational restraint. The next iteration should test model comparison behavior, mobile navigation depth and Vietnamese/English content parity with users, then replace reference photography with a fully owned asset set.
