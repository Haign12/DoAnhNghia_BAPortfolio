# Audience and Top Tasks

No direct user interviews, analytics or recruiter-session recordings were supplied. Audience statements below are therefore labeled by evidence strength.

## Audience 1 — Recruiter / Talent Acquisition

**Status:** EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE

Trigger/context:
- receives portfolio from CV/LinkedIn/application;
- needs a quick screen before forwarding to a design/product lead.

Top tasks:
1. Confirm role and level quickly.
2. Find 2–4 relevant projects without decoding the site.
3. Verify employment/education summary and CV.
4. Decide whether the work appears credible enough to shortlist.
5. Find contact details.

Questions / proof:
- “Is this clearly a UI/UX designer?” → explicit positioning.
- “What has this person actually designed?” → selected work with platform/role.
- “Does the portfolio show thinking, not only screens?” → decisions/constraints/outcome narrative.
- “Can I forward a concise proof set?” → stable case-study URLs + resume.

Success signal: reaches a relevant case study/CV and can make a shortlist decision with low ambiguity.

## Audience 2 — Design / Product Hiring Lead

**Status:** EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE

Trigger/context:
- opens a portfolio after recruiter handoff, referral or direct application;
- higher need for depth and evidence.

Top tasks:
1. Understand problem framing and role ownership.
2. See how decisions connect user/business need to interface choices.
3. Judge craft across hierarchy, interaction, system thinking and implementation awareness.
4. Detect whether outcomes are evidence-based or exaggerated.
5. Compare projects for range and depth.

Questions / proof:
- “What was the problem and constraint?” → case-study opening thesis.
- “What did Nghia personally own?” → role/scope/contribution.
- “Why this solution?” → decision chain + artifacts.
- “What changed or was learned?” → outcome/limitation/reflection.
- “Can this designer operate in real product constraints?” → state/system/accessibility/implementation notes where factual.

Success signal: enough confidence to interview/discuss a role.

## Audience 3 — Potential Client / Collaborator

**Status:** PROFESSIONAL_HYPOTHESIS

Trigger/context:
- receives a direct/shared link or discovers a project;
- evaluates aesthetic/strategic fit before contacting.

Top tasks:
1. Identify whether portfolio quality fits their ambition.
2. Find a relevant industry/project example.
3. Understand the designer’s approach and practical contribution.
4. Reach a low-friction contact route.

Success signal: sends a qualified email with project context.

## Top-task ranking across audiences

| Rank | Top task | User importance | Business consequence | Design implication |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decide whether the designer’s work is relevant and strong | very high | very high | selected work must appear immediately after positioning |
| 2 | Understand role, decisions and thinking inside a project | very high | very high | case-study pages are proof narratives, not screen galleries |
| 3 | Verify range/fit across projects | high | high | concise project metadata + distinct page roles |
| 4 | Verify background/CV | high for recruiter | high | resume remains persistent utility action |
| 5 | Contact after confidence is established | high | very high | email path visible but proof precedes stronger conversion moment |

## Entry contexts

- Homepage from application/LinkedIn/CV.
- Direct case-study URL shared by recruiter/candidate.
- External live project → return to portfolio.
- Search-engine deep entry to a case study.
- Resume → portfolio URL.

Design must therefore provide orientation/contact on case-study pages without forcing a home restart.

## Research basis

- Nielsen Norman Group portfolio hiring research: 200+ hiring managers; process/past work expectations vary by role/seniority.
- Interaction Design Foundation 2026 portfolio guidance: communicate both “what” and “how”; portfolio craft is itself evaluated; keep a focused set of relevant case studies.
- Existing project `content-gap-research-notes.md`: narrative gaps and no measured outcome evidence.

## Evidence boundary

No statement above is represented as direct research with this portfolio’s actual visitors. Phase 2+ should validate the top-task sequence using observed recruiter/design-lead review sessions if outcome validation is required.
