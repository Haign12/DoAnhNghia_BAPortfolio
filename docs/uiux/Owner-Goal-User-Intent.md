## Content/proof timing rules

- Do not ask for contact before the site has shown at least one meaningful proof path.
- Do not bury the CV; it is a recruiter utility action, not the site’s primary narrative.
- Do not fabricate numeric impact. When outcome data is absent, label qualitative output + limitation + next validation.
- Do not treat visual luxury as proof by itself; luxury is the presentation layer around credible evidence.
