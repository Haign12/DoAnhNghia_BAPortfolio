# Audience Intent & Top Tasks

No analytics, recruiter interview data, CRM or direct user research was supplied for this portfolio. Audience/task statements below are therefore **PROFESSIONAL_HYPOTHESIS** unless marked otherwise.

## Audience groups

| Audience | Typical trigger | Top task | Key question | Evidence needed | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recruiter / HR | Screening a candidate or opening a shared portfolio link | Decide quickly whether the profile fits an open UI/UX role | “Who is Nghĩa, what level/scope does he work at, and is the work relevant?” | Clear positioning, current experience, selected work, CV | Open CV / shortlist / contact |
| Hiring manager / Design Lead / Product Lead | Evaluating portfolio depth after initial screening | Judge design thinking, ownership, constraints and quality of decisions | “Can this designer explain why, not only show screens?” | Case-study narrative, role/scope, decisions, limitations, implemented work | Open flagship case study / contact |
| Founder / potential client | Needs a designer for website/product redesign | Decide fit, visual quality and ability to structure ambiguous work | “Can Nghĩa make complex products clear and deliver polished UI?” | Relevant projects, live work, capabilities, process cues | Email conversation |

## Top-task ranking

1. **Understand positioning in under one screen.**
2. **Find one highly relevant project fast.**
3. **Understand contribution, constraints and design decisions.**
4. **Verify experience / background without reading a long biography.**
5. **Reach contact or CV without hunting.**

## Question → evidence → action matrix

| User question | Evidence strategy | Objection | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is this a UI/UX/Product Designer portfolio or a general creative portfolio? | Explicit role line + product/system positioning | “Looks stylish but unclear what he does.” | Open selected work |
| Is the work real and relevant? | Distinguish personal concept, redesign and implemented live project; link to live work | “Concepts may overstate production impact.” | Open project/live site |
| Can he reason about UX? | Case study sections: context, role, constraint, decision, outcome/limitation | “Only polished mockups.” | Continue case study |
| Can he operate inside real constraints? | Experience timeline + implementation mentions + limitation honesty | “No evidence of delivery/collaboration.” | CV / contact |
| How do I reach him? | Email CTA repeated at high-intent moments | “Contact path is buried.” | `mailto:` |

## Homepage implications

- Start with role + point of view + direct path to work; do not lead with a long “about me”.
- Show 3–4 flagship projects at large scale with role/type/context metadata.
- Put experience/profile after proof, not before it.
- Keep CV/contact persistently discoverable in the top navigation.
- Do not use decorative service cards that imply freelance services not evidenced by the source.

## Case-study implications

- Entry must instantly show project type, role, scope/status and one defining visual.
- The first two scroll sections should answer: problem/context → decisions → output/limitations.
- Measured outcomes only when supplied; otherwise state qualitative outcome and validation gap.
- Each case study should offer a clear next-project or contact path at the end.

## Success definition for redesign

**User success:** a relevant visitor can identify Nghĩa’s role, find a relevant project, understand one meaningful design decision and reach a contact/CV action without uncertainty.

**Business success:** more qualified interview/project conversations is the intended outcome, but it remains **UNMEASURED** until analytics or manual lead attribution is available.
