02 / Evidence model

Credibility before seniority theater.

Personal projects are useful proof of judgment and execution, but they cannot manufacture production impact or organizational leadership. The portfolio labels what is implemented, what comes from benchmarks, what remains a hypothesis and what still needs real validation.

IMPLEMENTED

Working prototype

Real source code, responsive behavior, states and interactions that can be opened and inspected.

BENCHMARK

External pattern

Product/documentation evidence used to inform a decision — never presented as user research on my prototype.

HYPOTHESIS

Needs validation

A proposed relationship between design and user behavior. It stays a hypothesis until tested.

TARGET METRIC

What I would measure

Task success, decision time, error rate, confidence, adoption or guardrails defined before claiming improvement.

NOT CLAIMED

Fake impact removed

No invented conversion, revenue, retention, stakeholder quotes, user counts or production outcomes.

NEXT EVIDENCE

Baseline → iteration

Run the same critical tasks before and after iteration so design changes can be evaluated rather than narrated.

Evidence correction · 17 Sep 2026
The portfolio/CV previously contained numerical impact claims without repository-backed measurement evidence. Those claims are being removed rather than reframed as accomplishments.
